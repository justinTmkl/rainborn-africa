Before you say it

The four objections, answered

Flip a card.

Objection 1

"Sicily is way bigger."

True — and

25,711 km² against 781. We saw ours in a week and still want to go back. Bigger mostly means more places you didn't get to.

Objection 2

"Sicily has an active volcano."

Also true

Kefalonia had an earthquake in 1953 and, as a direct result, a very good bakery in Fiskardo now. Lower stakes, honestly.

Objection 3

"Sicily has 3,000 years of history."

Not arguing that one

Genuinely incredible, no notes. We're not debating the history — we're debating where you'd rather be at six with a frappé.

Objection 4

"Everyone's heard of Sicily."

Exactly

Myrtos was basically empty in August. Nobody's west-coast bay was. That's not a bug — that's the whole pitch.
